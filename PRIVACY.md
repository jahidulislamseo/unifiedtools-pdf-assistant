# Privacy Policy for UnifiedTools Pro - PDF & Document Assistant

**Last updated: July 27, 2026**

UnifiedTools Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle permissions and data within our Chrome Extension.

## 1. Zero Data Collection Policy
- **No Personal Data Collection:** UnifiedTools Pro - PDF & Document Assistant does **NOT** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user credentials, browsing history, or documents.
- **Local Operation:** All features, including page printing and PDF preparation, run entirely on your local machine.

## 2. Chrome Permissions Justification
- `activeTab`: Used to trigger native browser printing (`window.print()`) on the currently active tab when requested.
- `scripting`: Used to safely inject print commands into the active page DOM.
- `contextMenus`: Used to create right-click shortcuts for word counting, image conversion, and PDF link extraction.
- `tabs`: Used to launch corresponding tool pages on `unifiedtoolspro.xyz` when right-click actions or dashboard clicks are triggered.

## 3. Contact & Support
If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us via our official website support channels.
