Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions regarding security, document storage, or permissions? Here are direct answers.

Does this extension upload or read my sensitive documents? +
No. The extension operates entirely within your browser runtime. The clean webpage-to-PDF tool uses native browser printing mechanisms, and drag-and-drop transfers pass files in-memory to client utilities on UnifiedTools Pro without intermediate telemetry servers.
How does the clean webpage-to-PDF export differ from Chrome's print button? +
Standard Chrome print preserves sticky headers, video popups, sidebar banner ads, and cookie notices. UnifiedTools Pro PDF Assistant injects targeted CSS that hides all ad selectors and navigation wrappers before the print preview opens, yielding a clean document.
Can I customize the subfolder where documents are organized? +
Yes. The built-in Settings panel allows you to configure default export subfolders, toggle dark/light themes, and selectively enable or disable specific context menu items.
Is this extension free and open source? +
Yes, it is 100% free, open-source under the MIT license, and maintained on GitHub. You are free to inspect the source code, fork it, or build custom extensions on top of it.
